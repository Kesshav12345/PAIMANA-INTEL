# EXTERNAL DATA REQUIREMENTS

## WPI/PPI (Wholesale & Producer Price Indices)
- **Why required**: To distinguish nominal cost escalation from real cost escalation.
- **Source**: External Excel sheets provided in the repository (e.g. wpi_monthly_index_202608.xlsx).
- **Integration Strategy**: Map WPI commodity/industry codes to PAIMANA HML sectors (e.g. Transport -> WPI Transport Equipment/Fuel). Needs a crosswalk mapping.
