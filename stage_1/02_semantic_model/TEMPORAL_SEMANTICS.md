# TEMPORAL SEMANTICS

- **Reporting Period**: The month and year the report was generated (e.g., October 2025). This is the 'Observation Date'.
- **Cumulative vs Monthly**: 'Expenditure' is explicitly cumulative up to the reporting date. 'Physical Progress' is a point-in-time snapshot of cumulative progress.
- **Fiscal Year vs Calendar Year**: The reports generally state the calendar month (e.g., 'April 2025'), though government metrics may align with April-March fiscal cycles.
